# timely - turns your phone into a minimalist clock

this app quickly turns your phone into a very minimalist watch.

## features

- the program also has protection against burn-in, every 30 seconds the pixels shift
- for now, you can't customize your watch, but we'll be adding a settings feature in the next version.
- hours, seconds, minutes are shown

## requirements

- any android phone
- have Android 12 or higher
